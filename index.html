Our Story

Where Filipino Flavor Meets Food, Music & Fun

Manila Bistro is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place, a stage, and a table all in one. We bring the warmth of Filipino hospitality and the energy of Filipino celebration to Southern California.

"A little piece of Manila in San Diego — with all the food, music and Filipino hospitality, minus the traffic, smoke-belching buses and horn-blowing jeepneys!"

Our kitchen is built on recipes rooted in Filipino tradition — sinigang, lechon kawali, kare-kare, pancit — made with care and served with pride. Our stage brings music, karaoke, and entertainment that turns every night into a celebration.
